# Third-party audio

The piano samples in `assets/piano/` are selected notes from **Salamander Grand Piano V3**, recorded by Alexander Holm from a Yamaha C5 grand piano.

- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0
- Source: https://tonejs.github.io/audio/salamander/
- Project information: https://freepats.zenvoid.org/Piano/acoustic-grand-piano.html

The files are redistributed in MP3 form and are used as multisamples with playback-rate interpolation.
