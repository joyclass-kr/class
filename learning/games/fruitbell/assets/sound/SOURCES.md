# Fruit Bell sound sources

All short sound effects in `sfx/` are derived from CC0 assets and were converted to mono 48 kHz Ogg Vorbis for this site.

## Recorded bell and paper sounds

- Source pack: **100 CC0 SFX** by rubberduck
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-sfx
- License: CC0 1.0
- Used originals: `bell_01.ogg`–`bell_03.ogg`, `paper_01.ogg`–`paper_03.ogg`

## Game feedback sounds

- Source packs: **Interface Sounds** by Kenney
- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- License: CC0 1.0
- Used originals: `confirmation_001.ogg`, `confirmation_002.ogg`, `confirmation_004.ogg`, `error_003.ogg`, `error_005.ogg`, `drop_004.ogg`

## Processing

- High-pass filter at 70 Hz
- Loudness normalization by sound role
- 48 kHz, mono, Ogg Vorbis quality 4
